# Basic Rules

## Die Roll Conventions

* **"D":** Represents common six-sided dice. Unless noted otherwise, "2D" means rolling two dice and adding their results together.
* **DM (Dice Modifier):** A number added to or subtracted from a dice roll. For example, "2D, DM-2" means rolling two dice, adding them together, and subtracting 2.
* **D66:** Throw two six-sided dice without adding them together. Use one die for the "tens" place and the other for the "ones" place, yielding 36 possible results.

---

## The Basic Game Mechanic

The core mechanic of Cepheus Deluxe is the **throw** (dice throw). Throw 2D, add the relevant skill and characteristic DMs, and if the total equals or exceeds the target number, you succeed. For example, "throw Athletics/STR 8+" means throwing 2D, adding your Athletics skill level and Strength Dice Modifiers, and succeeding on a total of 8 or more.

* **Effect:** The amount by which your total throw exceeds or misses the target number. For instance, rolling an 11 against a target of 8 results in an Effect of $11 - 8 = 3$.
* **Automatic Success/Failure:** Regardless of target numbers or circumstances, if a task is even remotely feasible, a natural (unmodified) roll of 2 is always a failure, and a natural roll of 12 is always a success.
* **Opposed Throws:** When two characters oppose each other (e.g., a spy sneaking past a guard), each throws 2D plus appropriate skill and characteristic modifiers, and the higher roll wins. On a tie, the character with the higher total DM wins. If both roll the same Effect, reroll the tie.
* **Unskilled Characters:** Attempting a throw with a skill you do not possess incurs a **DM-3** penalty.
* **Trying Again:** Failed skill or characteristic throws can generally be retried, unless failure carries severe consequences (such as a Demolitions failure causing an explosion), which precludes second chances.
* **Routine Competence:** Skilled characters can usually practice their profession under normal conditions without rolling. Throws are reserved for serious risk, time pressure, opposition, or adverse conditions.

## Collaboration

Characters can work together on a task throw:
* The character with the highest combined skill and characteristic DMs makes the primary skill throw.
* An assistant can make a skill throw at the same difficulty; success grants **DM+1** to the primary character, while failure imposes no penalty.
* Only one direct assistant is allowed for the main throw, though other characters may assist the assistant at the same difficulty.
* Assisting characters do not necessarily need to use the exact same skill if the Referee agrees a related skill makes sense (e.g., an engineer throwing Engineering to assist a Pilot's skill throw).

---

## Advantage

In specific advantageous situations related to Traits and cybernetics, rules may grant **Advantage**: throw 3D and choose the 2 highest die results.

---

## Fatigue

Fatigue imposes a **DM-2** penalty to all throws. It can be removed with 8 hours of rest.

## Common Target Numbers

| Difficulty | Target Number | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Easy** | 6+ | Repairing a damaged vehicle system in a properly equipped workshop. |
| **Average** | 8+ | Shooting a target in Effective range. |
| **Difficult** | 10+ | Shooting a target beyond Effective range. |
| **Formidable** | 12+ | Landing a ship in a hurricane with a damaged engine, under enemy fire. |
| **Impossible** | 14+ | Anything that the Referee would find amusing. |
